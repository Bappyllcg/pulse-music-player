# Pulse Music Player 🎵

> A sleek, private, offline-first music player for your personal audio collection. Designed specifically for iPhone and modern smartphones.

---

## ✨ Why Pulse?

Most streaming apps require subscriptions, internet access, and constant logins. **Pulse** is built for people who own their music files and want a clean, ad-free listening experience that feels like a native Apple Music app.

- **100% Private & Local:** Your music never leaves your device. No servers, no tracking, and no accounts required.
- **True Offline Playback:** Works completely in Airplane Mode or without Wi-Fi / cellular data.
- **Lock Screen Controls:** Control playback, view artwork, skip tracks, and scrub right from your iPhone lock screen or Control Center.
- **Sleep Timer:** Fall asleep to your favourite songs without draining your battery overnight.

---

## 📱 How to Install on Your Phone

Pulse is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that installs directly from the web without going through the App Store.

### On iPhone (iOS Safari)
1. Open the website link in **Safari** (`https://bappyllcg.github.io/pulse-music-player/`).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square icon with an arrow pointing up at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down the menu and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
5. Open **Pulse** from your home screen — it will launch in full screen without any browser bars, just like a native iOS app!

### On Android (Chrome)
1. Open the website link in **Google Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** (⋮) in the top-right corner.
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm installation, then launch Pulse from your app drawer.

### On Mac / Windows / PC
- Open the link in **Chrome**, **Edge**, or **Brave**, then click the **Install** icon in the address bar to run Pulse in its own desktop window.

## 🎧 How to Use

### 1. Import Your Music
- Tap **+ Import MP3s** in the top header (or tap the center artwork card when your library is empty).
- Select one or multiple audio files from your **Files app**, **iCloud Drive**, or **Downloads** folder.
- Supported audio formats include:
  - **MP3** (`.mp3`)
  - **M4A / AAC** (`.m4a`, `.aac`)
  - **WAV** (`.wav`)
  - **FLAC & OGG** (`.flac`, `.ogg`)

### 2. Playing Music
- Tap any song in the list to start listening.
- Use the standard player controls to **Play/Pause**, **Skip**, or scrub through the track timeline.
- Use the **Shuffle** (🔀) and **Repeat** (🔁) buttons to customize queue playback.
- If your phone is locked, control playback directly using your phone's lock screen or headphones buttons.

### 3. Managing Your Library ("Up Next")
- Tap the **Up Next** button at the top of the Now Playing screen to view your active playlist.
- To remove a song, tap the **Remove icon** (⊖) next to the track title. *(This only removes it from Pulse — your original audio file on your device remains completely safe and untouched).*

### 4. Sleep Timer
- Tap the **🌙 Sleep** button next to *Up Next*.
- Choose how long you want music to play (**5, 10, 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes**) or select **End of current track**.
- The button will display a live countdown. When the timer finishes, playback smoothly pauses so your phone can sleep.

## 💡 Helpful Tips & FAQ

#### Where are my audio files stored?
Your files are stored safely inside your browser's private offline database (**IndexedDB**) directly on your device. Clearing your browser data/cache will reset your library.

#### Can I listen in Airplane Mode?
**Yes.** Once you have loaded the app and added your songs, everything (the app shell and audio files) works 100% offline without any network connection.

#### Can I listen with the screen locked or while using other apps?
**Yes.** Pulse supports background audio playback. You can lock your screen or switch to other apps and your audio will continue playing uninterrupted.
